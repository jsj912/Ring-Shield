# R12: injected pattern shift (amends spec v1.1, Section 8.3)

**Drift was injected, not observed.** AMLworld is synthetic and contains no natural drift; every R12
table says so.

## Registered 2026-10-24, before any R12 run

Nothing below was chosen after seeing an R12 result. This replaces the v1.0 design in
`platform/experiments/r12_injected_shift.py` (days 8–17, pattern rows moved forward 5 days, daily
monitor steps).

### Decisions

1. **Injection.** CYCLE truth transactions are **removed from test day 8**; **day 9 is unchanged**,
   so CYCLE activity "returns" on day 9. No timestamps are invented. Removal matches truth rows to
   scored edges by sender, receiver and timestamp.
2. **Scope.** The pooled test graph goes into **one dedicated, clearly labelled experiment tenant**
   that no bank user can see. A single bank holds too few CYCLE rings to measure anything. The
   tenant's invisibility is **checked under row-level security by querying it as a bank-12 user**,
   and the result is reported.
3. **Monitor, exactly as specified.** ADWIN (`river`) on the **hourly mean and 99th percentile of
   calibrated scores**, **48 hourly steps over days 8–9**, with **day 8 as the reference**, δ from
   `l6.drift.delta` (0.002). The report gives the detection delay in hours after the start of day 9,
   or **"not detected"**.
4. **No retrain in R12.** A retrain at the end of day 9 could only be evaluated on rings it trained
   on. R11 already demonstrates the retrain loop, and R7 covers unseen-pattern recall. Instead R12
   reports **CYCLE ring recall on days 8–9 for the R7 CYCLE-held-out model against the full model**,
   descriptive only, citing R7.
5. **Model.** The R7 CYCLE-held-out ring layer, `experiments/R7_l3only_cycle/runs/full/seed1`
   exported to `models/artifacts/l3only_cycle_full_seed1` (`r12.model_dir`); the full model is the
   shipped `models/artifacts/full_gine_causal_seed1`. Detector seed 1.
6. **Simulator accuracy.** Not used: there is no retrain, so no simulated verdicts are needed.
7. **Positive control (synthetic).** The same 48-hour stream is replayed with a **synthetic step
   added to the monitor's input from hour 12 of day 9** (step 36 of 48): **+3 standard deviations of
   day 8's hourly means, on both statistics** (each statistic shifted by 3 × the standard deviation of
   its own 24 day-8 hourly values). The report says whether ADWIN fires and after how many hours. It
   is labelled synthetic in every output. **If ADWIN does not fire on the control, R12 stops and
   reports that the monitor is broken.**

### Expectation, stated before running

Test day 8 holds 654,467 scored transactions (about 24,000 per hour) and 27 CYCLE transactions;
day 9 holds 208,325 transactions and 28 CYCLE transactions. That is **~1 CYCLE transaction per
~24,000 per hour; we don't expect the monitor to fire on the real run.** "Not detected" is therefore
the expected result, and it means that a score-distribution monitor cannot see a pattern this sparse,
not that the monitor is broken; the positive control (decision 7) is what tests the monitor itself.

## Result (2026-10-24): stopped at the positive control; the monitor is broken

**Drift was injected, not observed.** Run: `python -m experiments.r12_injected_shift --pattern CYCLE`
from `platform/`; wall time 0.9 s (3 s with start-up). Outputs in `results/platform/r12/registered/`
(`monitor_real.md`, `monitor_control.md`, `run.json`).

| stream | step | detected | delay |
|---|---|---|---|
| real: CYCLE removed from day 8, day 9 unchanged | CYCLE returns at hour 0 of day 9 | **no** | **not detected** |
| control (**synthetic**): +3 SD from hour 12 of day 9 | mean +0.000669, p99 **+0.000000** | **no** | **not detected** |

**The synthetic control did not fire, so as registered (decision 7) R12 stops here and reports that
the monitor is broken.** The experiment tenant, the RLS check and the CYCLE recall table (decisions 2
and 4) were not run. Two causes, both measured:

1. **river's `ADWIN(clock=32)` tests for a change once every 32 updates.** In 48 hourly steps it
   tests once, at step 32, before the control's step at step 37 begins. In live daily operation it
   would test once a month, so the platform monitor cannot fire inside the ten-day test split.
2. **The hourly 99th percentile of calibrated scores is flat**: 0.015064 in all 24 day-8 hours
   (isotonic calibration maps the top of the range onto a plateau), so its standard deviation is 0
   and the control's "+3 SD" on p99 is +0. That statistic cannot move.

The real run's "not detected" is what was expected before running, but it is **not evidence** of
anything here: a monitor that cannot detect the control cannot be said to have missed the injection.

**Recorded difference from the registration.** The registration counted 27 day-8 CYCLE transactions;
those 27 truth rows match **31 scored edges**, because 4 other edges share a sender, receiver and
timestamp with a CYCLE transaction, and the removal (decision 1) removed all 31. Four extra edges out
of 654,467 on day 8; it does not affect the result.

**Next:** fixing the monitor is future work (`docs/FUTURE_IMPLEMENTATIONS.md`, F8). A changed monitor
needs a new R12 registration; this stopped run stays as reported.

## Diagnosis, 2026-10-25: why the positive control did not fire

The run above stays as reported (`results/platform/r12/registered/`, unchanged).

**What ran.** river 0.22.0 `ADWIN(delta=0.002)` (`platform/jobs/drift_monitor.py`), every other
parameter at river's default: `clock=32`, `max_buckets=5`, `min_window_length=5`,
`grace_period=10`. Two independent streams of **48 hourly values** (test days 8-9): the hourly mean
and the hourly 99th percentile of calibrated scores, in **raw units** (no standardisation). The
control added +0.000669 to the mean from step 37 (hour 12 of day 9) and +0 to the p99, whose 24
day-8 values are all 0.015064.

**How ADWIN decides** (`river/drift/adwin_c.pyx`). It tests only when `tick % clock == 0` and
`width > grace_period`. A test cuts the window into an older part (n0 values) and a newer part (n1)
at every bucket boundary with n0, n1 >= 5, and fires when |mean0 - mean1| > epsilon, with
epsilon = sqrt(2 m var delta') + (2/3) delta' m, m = 1/(n0-4) + 1/(n1-4),
delta' = ln(2 ln(width) / delta).

**Replay.** `platform/experiments/adwin_trace.py` is an instrumented copy of that code; it matches
river's width and detection at every step on 80 test streams with 88 detections, and on both R12
streams. Over every cut evaluated at every tested step (`results/platform/r12/diagnosis/summary.json`):

| config | stream | steps tested | largest \|mean diff\| at any cut | smallest epsilon at any cut | fired |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| as run, `clock=32` | mean | **step 32 only** (before the step at 37) | 0.000316 | 0.9061 | no |
| as run, `clock=32` | p99 | step 32 only | 0.000081 | 0.9058 | no |
| `clock=1` | mean | every step from 11 to 48 | 0.001663 | 0.5517 | no |
| `clock=1` | p99 | every step from 11 to 48 | 0.001769 | 0.5524 | no |

**Why it did not fire. Two causes, each sufficient on its own:**

1. **`clock=32` on 48 points.** ADWIN tested once, at step 32, five steps before the control's step
   begins. Nothing after the step was ever tested.
2. **The bound cannot be met on this stream.** epsilon's second term, (2/3) delta' m, does not depend
   on the data: it assumes values in [0, 1]. With at most 48 points it is at least 0.55 at every
   cut, while the hourly means are 0.001-0.003 and the control's step is 0.00067. Testing every hour
   does not help: the largest difference at any cut is over 300 times below the smallest threshold. The p99 stream is constant on day 8, so its "+3 SD" step is 0 and it cannot move.

The full per-step trace (tested or not, window sizes, every cut's n0, n1, mean difference and
epsilon) is in `results/platform/r12/diagnosis/trace_control_clock32.json` and
`trace_control_clock1.json`.

## Amendment, 2026-10-25, after the control failed

Registered after the diagnosis above and **before** any run below. Disclosure: the `clock=1` replay
of the hourly control in the diagnosis table was seen before this amendment was written; the real
stream under `clock=1` and the per-transaction arm (B) were not.

**A. Corrected config: `clock=1`, everything else unchanged** (`delta=0.002`, `max_buckets=5`,
`min_window_length=5`, `grace_period=10`, raw units). Reason: with river's default `clock=32` a
48-step stream is tested once, at step 32, before the control's step; the registered design
(decision 3) needs a test after every step. Rerun on the hourly stream for the real run and the
control. As the diagnosis shows, the hourly control is not expected to fire under A either.

**B. The hourly result is a finding, and a second arm is registered.** Finding: on 48 hourly values
ADWIN's bound has a data-independent floor of at least 0.55 per cut, so **no shift in hourly
calibrated-score statistics can be detected in two days, at any clock**. Second arm: **the same
monitor (A) on the per-transaction calibrated-score stream for days 8-9**, in timestamp order (ties
by edge_id): the real stream with CYCLE removed from day 8, and the control with the same
+0.000669 added to every calibrated score from hour 12 of day 9. Mean monitor only: a 99th
percentile is not defined for a single transaction. Reported: whether ADWIN fires, the first
detection's transaction and hour, and the delay in hours after the step (control) or after the
start of day 9 (real). Drift is injected, not observed, and the control is synthetic.

**Outputs.** For the admin R12 panel: `results/platform/r12/amended/panel.json` with the hourly
series (hour, mean, p99, ADWIN state) and the events for the real run and the control, for the
original run (`clock=32`, from the recorded tables), A, and B. For the supervisor drift screen:
`results/platform/r12/amended/tenant_hourly.json`, the real hourly series for banks 1, 20, 11, 118
and 12 (no synthetic step), with ADWIN under A, in the API's drift field names.

## Result of the amendment (2026-10-25)

**Drift was injected, not observed; the control is synthetic.** Run:
`python -m experiments.r12_amended` from `platform/`; wall time 152 s. The `clock=32` replay
reproduces the recorded run (neither stream fired) before anything new is reported. Outputs in
`results/platform/r12/amended/` (`summary.json`, `panel.json`, `tenant_hourly.json`).

| arm | stream | detections | after the onset | delay |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| A, hourly, `clock=1` | real (CYCLE returns at hour 0 of day 9) | 0 | no | **not detected** |
| A, hourly, `clock=1` | control (**synthetic**, +0.000669 from hour 12 of day 9) | 0 | no | **not detected** |
| B, per transaction, `clock=1` | real | 2: day 8 12:21, day 9 11:09 | day 9 11:09 | 11.15 h after day 9 starts |
| B, per transaction, `clock=1` | control (**synthetic**) | 2: day 8 12:21, day 9 11:09 | **none** | **not detected** |

**The control did not fire in either arm, so R12 still cannot report a detection** (decision 7 of
the registration): a monitor that misses the control cannot be said to have seen the injection.

* **A** is the finding the amendment predicted: on 48 hourly values the bound's floor (at least 0.55)
  is far above any hourly shift in calibrated scores.
* **B**: the real and control streams are identical until 12:00 on day 9, and both detections fall
  before it, so **neither is a response to the injection or to the control**: they are changes in
  the ordinary score stream (the day 8 one is before CYCLE's removal could matter in either
  direction). The 11:09 detection reset the window 51 minutes before the control's step, leaving
  6,702 transactions of reference against 95,775 after the step. With that split epsilon is about
  0.0020 at the end of day 9 (day 8's per-transaction SD 0.0186), three times the step: the step is
  0.036 per-transaction SDs, too small to resolve against 51 minutes of reference.
* The real stream's 11:09 detection is therefore not a 11.15-hour detection delay for CYCLE. The
  delay column reports what the registered rule computes, and this paragraph says why it does not
  measure the injection.

**Per-tenant hourly series** (banks 1, 20, 11, 118, 12; real scores, no synthetic step; ADWIN as in
A): 48 hours each, no events. `tenant_hourly.json` uses the API's drift field names (`day`, `mean`,
`p99`) with hourly steps.

**What stays open:** a monitor that can see a shift of this size needs a reference long enough for
the bound and no reset just before the change; any such change needs a new registration
(`docs/FUTURE_IMPLEMENTATIONS.md`, F8).

## R12b: registered 2026-11-12, run 2026-11-12 (`docs/deep-analysis.md` #6)

**Drift was injected, not observed; the control is synthetic.** A new registration was written in
`docs/deep-analysis.md` #6 before any R12b code ran. It keeps the R12 stream (seed-1 scored edges,
the same **31** CYCLE transactions removed from day 8, 48 hourly steps, control onset at hour 12
of day 9) and changes only the monitor and the statistics:

- **Statistics:** S1, the hourly share of edges above the seed cutoff; S2, the hourly raw-score
  p99. Both are standardised on day 8.
- **Monitors:** upper CUSUM (k 0.5, h 4) and Page–Hinkley (δ 0.5, λ 4), from day 9, hour 0.
- **Ladder:** +1 / +3 / +5 SD.
- **False-alarm bound:** no alarm in-sample on day 8 and none on day 9 before the onset.
- **Success:** an eligible arm detects +3 SD within 6 h.

**Result: failure as registered.** No arm is eligible:

| statistic | monitor | false alarms (day 8 / day 9 before onset) | +3 SD |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 above-cutoff share | CUSUM | 17 / 26, 29, 32, 34, 35 | fires within 1 h, but on the pre-existing shift |
| S1 above-cutoff share | Page–Hinkley | 17 / 26, 29, 32, 34, 35 | same |
| S2 raw p99 | CUSUM | undefined (day-8 SD 0) / 34 | degenerate |
| S2 raw p99 | Page–Hinkley | undefined / none | not detected |

**Why, measured:**

1. **2.9 % of day 8's raw scores share one tied value, 0.079603**, spanning p99 to p99.5, so the
   hourly p99 is constant on day 8. That is the same failure as the calibrated p99 (0.015064)
   above, and it comes from the detector's output, not from calibration.
2. **Day 9's above-cutoff share is 0.096 % against 0.031 % on day 8 (3.1×)**, on a third of the
   volume. Standardised on day 8, day 9 is at **+3.2 SD before any step**, larger than the
   control. S1 also has a diurnal cycle (lag-1 autocorrelation 0.60), which gives the in-sample
   alarm.

ADWIN's 0.55 floor was one obstacle. Replacing ADWIN exposes two more that no monitor choice
removes. **R12 is closed as not testable on HI-Small's primary window**, rather than "monitor
broken". A valid design needs a reference that contains the monitored regime, for example several
days with a per-hour-of-day baseline. **PR-43 stays not met.**

Script: `platform/experiments/r12b_cusum.py`. Output: `results/platform/r12b/summary.json`.
